What it is

OpenMath is discovery-first and literally infinite. Its flagship exhibit is a real interactive Mandelbrot explorer: the whole set on open, tap or drag to zoom forever — every zoom is a new world that was always inside one formula, z → z² + c, again and again. Every concept enters as a toy you can touch — Mandelbrot, Julia sets, Sierpinski, Koch snowflake, nature's fractals — and the depth ladder carries each learner, ages 3 to 90, as far as they want. Its founding shape is Dawn's own: Qin Shi Huang standardised the script and the axle width — one standardised seed, then let it branch infinitely. Math was never hard; it was taught backwards.

END GOAL

Math so simple that anyone can understand it — a 3-year-old can learn math and love it, and the depth ladder carries every learner, ages 3–90, as far as they want to go.
